Director's Message ISO 9001:2015 Certified

"Our mission is to ensure every family in Bihar receives ethical, world-class healthcare with genuine human compassion."

Welcome to Naman Hospital. When we founded this healthcare institution at Kankarbagh in Patna, our core belief was simple yet uncompromising: healthcare is not just a medical infrastructure, but a sacred covenant between doctors and families in their most critical moments.

Under our clinical stewardship, Naman Hospital has developed into an ISO 9001:2015 Certified tertiary institution equipped with round-the-clock 24x7 Critical Care Emergency & Trauma, Patna's dedicated Specialized Burn Care Unit, advanced modular operation theatres, computerized pathology, and 24-hour patient amenities.

Along with our multidisciplinary team of over 25+ Super-Specialists (MCh, DM, MS, MD, MDS), my personal pledge as Director is to ensure that every patient who walks through our doors is treated with absolute clinical precision, ethical transparency, dignity, and utmost care. We are honoured to be your trusted wellness partner.
