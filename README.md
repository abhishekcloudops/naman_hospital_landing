# Naman Hospital — Official Website

Official modern and fully responsive website for **Naman Hospital**, an ISO 9001:2015 certified multi-specialty healthcare institution located in Kankarbagh, Patna, Bihar.

---

## Key Highlights

- **24x7 Emergency & Trauma**: Round-the-clock emergency medical response, advanced ICU & NICU facilities, and dedicated ambulance service (`0612–3507140` / `+91 6201070884`).
- **20+ Clinical Departments**: Dedicated Burn Care Unit, Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement, Neurology & Spine Surgery, Laparoscopic Surgery, Urology & Dialysis, Gastroenterology, Oncology, Gynaecology, Paediatrics, and complete diagnostics.
- **Director**: Dr. Rajan Kumar (Director & Chief Consultant, MBBS, MD).
- **Location**: G-62/C, Rajni Path, Dr. R. N. Singh Road, Kankarbagh, Patna – 800 020.

## Website Structure

- [`index.html`](index.html): Home page featuring campus visual, 24x7 emergency status, Director's vision message, key stats, and quick appointment booking.
- [`services.html`](services.html): Complete breakdown of all 20+ super-specialty departments with interactive category filter tabs.
- [`doctors.html`](doctors.html): 25+ specialist directory with real-time keyword search, specialty filter pills, and WhatsApp appointment booking.
- [`contact.html`](contact.html): Interactive map, emergency contact list, 24x7 operational hours table, and appointment consultation request form.
- [`css/global.css`](css/global.css): Global design system, modern typography, color palette, and mobile-first responsive rules.
- [`js/main.js`](js/main.js): Navigation state, mobile hamburger menu, interactive counters, and scroll animations.

## Responsive Support

Fully tested and optimized for:
- Mobile phones (320px – 480px)
- Tablets & iPads (768px – 1024px)
- Desktop & large screens (1200px+)
- Fixed mobile bottom emergency dock for fast 1-tap calling and WhatsApp booking.
